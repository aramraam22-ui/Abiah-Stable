Abiah Equestrian Club 🐎

Where passion meets the spirit of authentic Arabian horsemanship


Abiah Stable was founded in 2010 to be a destination for lovers of equestrianism and purebred Arabian horses. We strive to provide a professional and safe environment for training , The stable includes a team of certified trainers with extensive experience in training both horses and riders of all levels from beginners to professionals.

Our website makes it easy for you to book appointments, explore our services, and contact us directly without the need for an in-person visit every time.



Our Vision :

Facilities and Environment 🏇 :

The stable includes training arenas equipped with the latest tools and air-conditioned, comfortable stalls for the horses. We also provide outdoor training areas and green spaces that allow riders and horses to train freely and safely



Project Idea 🐎 :

This project aims to automate the booking system in the stable. Through this system, visitors can easily schedule training sessions, view available time slots, and receive booking confirmation notifications. This step helps reduce errors and improves time management for both trainers and clients.
